Whitey's Crash Course

Two days, one hour each. Then it's yours.

Shakes & Malts 20 min

Highest frequency
  • Malt = shake + 1 heaping spoon malt powder (that's the only difference — malts are sweeter).
  • Build order: 2 scoops Malt Mix (MM) first → then flavoring → mix fast. Add MM ASAP so you don't overmix.
  • Regular flavors by size (S/M/L): 1½ / 2 / 3 scoops. Cut back: 1 / 1½ / 2.
  • Hot Fudge shakes (S/M/L): 2 / 3 / 4. Cut back: 1½ / 2 / 3.
  • Banana shake: dash of vanilla + small/medium/medium banana by size. With flavor: cut back flavoring, always ½ banana.
  • Thin shake requested? Ask how thin, then add milk accordingly.
Memory trick: "1½ – 2 – 3" is the default scoop ladder for regular size shakes — it echoes across sodas and flavored shakes too.

Sundaes 20 min

Highest frequency
  • Chocolate, Butterscotch, Marshmallow = UOJ (use your own judgment).
  • Fruit flavors — Strawberry, Pineapple, Peach, Red Raspberry (1/2/3 dip): 1½ / 2 / 3. Use plenty of fruit.
  • Hot Fudge (1/2/3 dip): 3 / 5 / 7.
  • Cup rule: 1 Dip → one dip cup; 2 Dip → two dip cup; 3 Dip → large shake cup or boat.
  • All sundaes must be weighed. Default to vanilla ice cream unless told otherwise — no upcharge for other flavors.

Ice Cream Sodas 20 min

Frequent
  • 1 Dip → medium cup. 2 & 3 Dip → large cup.
  • Order: flavoring in first → stir with a metal spoon → vanilla ice cream added over the sink → top off with carbonated water.
  • Flavorings ending in -BERRY or -APPLE (1/2/3 dip): 4 / 5 / 5. All other flavorings: 2 / 3 / 3.
  • Half and Half is always 2 Tbsp, regardless of size.
  • Fill line: "top of the i" in the Whitey's logo for 1 & 2 dip; "top of the Ice Cream" wording for 3 dip.
  • Small soda in large cup: use the large recipe/procedure but only one scoop of ice cream, charge for 1 dip, press "small soda in large cup" button.

Banana Splits 20 min

Frequent
  • Banana always goes on before toppings — no exceptions.
  • 1 Dip: 6oz vanilla + half a medium banana, same topping amount as a 1 Dip sundae.
  • 2 Dip: 4oz vanilla + 4oz strawberry, whole banana, 1 scoop pineapple + 1 scoop strawberry topping.
  • 3 Dip (traditional): adds 4oz chocolate dip + ½ pump chocolate topping to the 2 Dip build.
  • Stacking order bottom to top: ice cream dip(s) → sliced banana → fruit toppings (matched to their own flavor dip) → whipped cream → nuts → cherry.
  • Boat version: same toppings/amounts as the cup — just cut the banana lengthwise instead of slicing thin.

Special Sundaes & Bostons 20 min

Frequent
  • Special sundaes: 1 Dip → 8oz cup; 2 Dip → 16oz cup (unless otherwise noted).
  • Learn the shape, not every number — Turtle (butterscotch + hot fudge + pecans), S'mores (marshmallow + hot fudge + choc chips, graham ice cream), Cherry Bon Bon (hot fudge + black cherry), Dusty Road (chocolate + malt powder), Brownie Under (brownie on bottom, then ice cream + fudge, repeat for 2 dip).
  • Bostons = shake/malt + a sundae on top. Large only.
  • 5 Rules for Bostons: 1) large only 2) use the large shake recipe 3) fill to the apostrophe 4) 4oz dip on top 5) 1 scoop/pump topping (2 for Hot Fudge).
  • If the shake portion uses a flavored ice cream, press the "Ice Cream Boston" button in addition to one of the three Boston buttons.

Freezes, Floats, Cones & Packages 20 min

Lower frequency — just get the gist
  • Regular Freeze: large only. 2 large dips + 4 MM + a touch of carbonated water, mixed and spun off in stages.
  • Sherbet Freeze comes in all 3 sizes (S/M/L dips: 2/3/4; cut back flavoring: 1/1.5/2).
  • Pop float: fill cup with pop, carefully add ice cream over sink, top off with more pop.
  • Slush float: fill ¼–½ with slush, add vanilla ice cream, top with more slush around the edges.
  • Cone weights: Mini 2oz, One Dip 4-5oz, Two Dip 8oz, Three Dip 12oz, Flavor Flight = four 2oz dips in a banana boat.
  • Pup cup = mini dip of vanilla + dog bone on top, mini dip cup.
  • Packages: Pints and Quarts can be hand-packed; 1.5 Quarts and 4 Qt pails are prepackaged only.
